Read this before calling it a violation

A MassHealth provider number and a corporate charter are separate instruments. 130 CMR 450.212 sets out ten provider eligibility criteria, and none of them requires active corporate registration, good standing, or notice to MassHealth of a dissolution. 130 CMR 450.231 conditions payment on the provider being a participating provider on the date of service and says nothing about corporate status. On the face of those two sections, paying a dissolved company breaks no MassHealth rule. MGL c.156C §70(c) addresses the company: a dissolved LLC continues in existence but shall not carry on any business except winding up. Whether billing during the window fits that limit is for the Secretary or a court to decide, and this page does not decide it. Two of the Commonwealth's registers disagree and nothing in the billing regulation requires them to agree. A legislator can close that gap; an auditor cannot.

Against Massachusetts peers

The peer set is every billing NPI whose NPPES practice location is in Massachusetts: 11,107 NPIs that billed any of these codes nationally were looked up one by one, and 635 are in Massachusetts. A high percentile is a pattern to explain.

Adult foster care, S5140: high within the peer range

Beyond Independent Living's caseload rose from 58 to 204 beneficiaries a month between 2018 and 2024. In every year its paid per beneficiary-month sat above the Massachusetts median, between the 82nd and 95th percentile ($2,280–$2,588 against yearly medians of $2,098–$2,361). In the median month about one in nine Massachusetts S5140 billers sat higher, so it sits at the high end of the range. After March 2022, when nearly every Massachusetts biller moved to daily lines, it was paid $113.41 a line in 2023 against a median of $82.62, while sending fewer lines per patient-month than most (21–23 against a median of 28–30).

Checked and not an anomaly: its claim lines per patient roughly quadrupled in March 2022 while pay per patient held flat. Under a strict test, 18 Massachusetts adult foster care billers, this one among them, made the same switch that month, and 33 of the 41 that still billed in coarse lines in January 2022 had switched by July. It is a statewide change in how claims were itemised.

Home health and group adult foster care: mostly ordinary

Beyond Healthcare Agency's aide visits (G0156) paid per patient-month sat between the 35th and 52nd percentile. Its H0043 group adult foster care billing sat just below the Massachusetts median per patient-month in 2018 ($1,108 against $1,149, the 34th percentile of 82 billers). Read this before publishing anything

  • None of this establishes fraud. An involuntary dissolution is a failure to file an annual report, not a finding of wrongdoing. The auditor's phrase is “appear to be unallowable”. A high peer percentile is a pattern, not a finding. Every sentence that goes out should survive being read by a lawyer for the other side.
  • Do not add figures across sources. HHS Medicaid billing and the Comptroller's ledger overlap; Medicare, the SBA and the Provider Relief Fund are separate programs. Each tab keeps them apart for that reason.
  • Do not read the clustered dissolution dates as a single event. For LLCs and business corporations, MGL c.156C §70 and c.156D §14.21 each prescribe written notice and a 90-day cure period, and neither names a date. The nine nonprofits dissolved on 30 June 2025 fall under a different chapter, which this check did not read. The clustering is an observed batch pattern in the filing data, not a published schedule. Report the count, not the coincidence of dates.
  • $105.9M is a floor. The Comptroller's ledger records its own disbursements. HHS billing data record more Medicaid money for these providers than the ledger does. Payments by managed-care plans never appear in the ledger under a provider's name and would explain the gap (inference: neither file carries a payer flag). Medicare pays through CMS.
  • The appropriation labels are accounting buckets. “Managed Care Plan”, “MassHealth Senior Care” and “Indemnity / Third Party Liability Plan” describe which account the money left, not which service was billed.
  • Other people share these surnames. The Osagiede search returns filings by at least a dozen other individuals, including an attorney, a shipping company and several churches. Only the 45 entities on the Register tab carry Naomi Osagiede or Naomi Egah as an officer, manager, agent or signatory. Other people are named on this page only where a public filing ties them to a cluster transaction.
